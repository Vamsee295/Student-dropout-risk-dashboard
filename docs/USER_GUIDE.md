# User Guide

Welcome to the EduRisk platform. This guide covers how to use the system depending on your assigned role.

## 1. Student Guide
As a student, the platform helps you stay on track academically.

- **Login**: Navigate to the homepage, select the "Student" role, and log in with your university credentials.
- **Dashboard**: The main dashboard displays your current CGPA, attendance rate, and real-time risk score.
- **View Recommendations**: If your risk score is elevated, an AI assistant will provide customized advice (e.g., "Attend the next 3 lab sessions to improve your standing").
- **Notifications**: Look for the bell icon in the top right to see messages from your faculty advisor.

## 2. Faculty Guide
As a faculty member, you use the platform to monitor your assigned students and provide timely interventions.

- **Login**: Select the "Faculty" role.
- **Dashboard**: You will see an aggregate view of your cohort's performance.
- **At-Risk Students Panel**: Students with a risk score > 70% are highlighted in red.
- **Logging Interventions**: 
  1. Click on an at-risk student's profile.
  2. Review the SHAP analysis to understand *why* they are at risk (e.g., low attendance vs. poor grades).
  3. Click "Log Intervention" to schedule a meeting or send a warning.
- **Attendance & Grades**: Updates to university LMS systems sync automatically with this dashboard.

## 3. Dean Guide
As a dean, your goal is macro-level oversight and university-wide retention.

- **Analytics Dashboard**: View aggregate data across all departments.
- **Department Comparison**: Identify which departments have the highest dropout risks.
- **KPI Dashboard**: Monitor real-time retention metrics.
- **Reports**: Use the "Export" button to generate PDF or Excel reports for accreditation boards and university management.
